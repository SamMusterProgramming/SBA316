Personality vs Nature

Certain aspects of personality show significant correlation with landscape preferences: people classified as less “emotionally stable” prefer those landscapes containing structural rhythms and recurrent “patterns”, even sacrificing the universally accepted quality of vegetation spontaneity and vigour. People with high scores with regard to “sense of responsibility” tend to reject hostile, defoliated or wintery landscapes, despite their greater legibility. These facts confirm previous results concerning personality-landscape preference relationships, and coincide with the ample bibliography available in relation to personality and aesthetic judgement in general. The affective system brought into play in landscape appraisal is a consequence of wider personal strategies concerning an individual's attitude towards the world and his fellow human beings. The conclusion reached is that the environmental perception of others deserves respect in the planning process.

Personality vs Music

Personality and music preferences are complexly related, and there are many factors that can influence how people experience music and the world around them Music can influence how people experience nature, such as when relaxing music enhances the experience of viewing a landscape from a car. Music can also change how people perceive urban environments, and the arousal levels in music can alter how people perceive outdoor environments
